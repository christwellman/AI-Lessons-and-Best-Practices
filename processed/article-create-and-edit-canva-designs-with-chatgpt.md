## Summary

This document explains how to use the ChatGPT Canva connector. You can create personalized designs in bulk with AI agents. ChatGPT navigates Canva and fills templates with custom text variations. You must review and clean up the final designs manually.

## Key Takeaways

- Create a template on canva.com/ai before you start.
- Enable Agent Mode in ChatGPT and connect your Canva account.
- Prompt ChatGPT to find your template and make custom versions.
- ChatGPT duplicates the template and adds personalized text.
- Review the designs in Canva and fix formatting before download.

## Techniques / Prompts Extracted

- Find my [template name] in Canva and create 4 versions with custom messages for: Michael (bodybuilding win), Anna (new baby), etc.
