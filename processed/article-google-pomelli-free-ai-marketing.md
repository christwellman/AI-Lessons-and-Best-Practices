# Free AI Marketing Team with Google Pomelli

> Source: pasted content
> Saved: 2026-06-01

Google's Pomelli (labs.google.com/pomelli) is a free tool that generates a full brand kit — product photos, website, and social media campaigns — from just your URL or a few product images. No agency or budget required, takes ~15 minutes.

## Workflow

1. Go to [labs.google.com/pomelli](https://labs.google.com/pomelli) → "Let's get started"
2. Drop in your website URL or 2–3 product photos → generates **Business DNA** (fonts, colors, brand voice, tagline)
3. Add product to Catalog → "Create photo shoot" for AI-generated product images
4. Click "Websites" → auto-generates a full site from your brand kit
5. Go to "Campaigns" → select product + aspect ratio, describe promo → get 5 ready-to-post ad images (animatable into videos)

## Campaign prompt template

> "I'm running a [holiday] special. [X]% off. Create a campaign targeting [your audience]."
