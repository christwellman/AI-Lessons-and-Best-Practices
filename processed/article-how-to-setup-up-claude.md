How to Set Up Claude in 2 Hours and Save Weeks of Repetitive Work
r/BusinessGrowthSystem - How to Set Up Claude in 2 Hours and Save Weeks of Repetitive Work
Spend 2 hours setting Claude up properly, and you could save yourself weeks of repetitive work.

The mistake most people make is treating Claude like a chatbot. They open it, type a prompt, get an answer, close it, and repeat the same process tomorrow. A better approach is to build an AI workspace that already understands your work, your preferences, your projects, and your workflows.

Here is a simple setup:

Install the Claude Desktop App

Use the desktop app when you want access to features such as Claude Code and local workflows.

Choose the Right Plan

If you use Claude heavily, a paid plan can give you higher usage limits and access to more capable models.

Use more capable models for complex work and faster models for simpler tasks.

Create a Workspace

Organize your files into simple folders such as:

• About Me
• Projects
• Templates
• Outputs
• References

A clean structure makes it much easier to find information and reuse previous work.

Give Claude Context

Don't expect Claude to understand your business from a single prompt.

Give it documents that explain:

• Who you are
• What your company does
• Your audience
• Your writing style
• Brand guidelines
• Your goals
• Your preferred workflows

Better context usually produces better results.

Build Your Personal Profile

Ask Claude to interview you.

Let it ask about your work, responsibilities, audience, communication style, preferences, and goals.

Then save the useful information as reference material that Claude can use in future projects.

Configure Project Instructions

Give each project clear instructions about:

• Tone
• Formatting
• Audience
• Rules
• Required information
• Words and phrases to avoid

The advantage is simple: you define the rules once instead of repeating them in every conversation.

Match the Model to the Task

Don't use the most powerful model for everything.

Use stronger reasoning models for:

• Coding
• Research
• Strategy
• Complex analysis
• Difficult writing

Use faster models for:

• Emails
• Summaries
• Brainstorming
• Simple rewriting
• Everyday tasks

Organize Your Knowledge

Keep your notes, prompts, templates, research, and documentation organized in one place.

You can use Obsidian, Notion, or another knowledge management tool.

The specific app matters less than having a consistent system.

Start Every Project With Context

Instead of saying:

"Write a blog post."

Give Claude the information it needs first:

"Review my project files, understand my audience and goals, identify the important points, and then help me write the blog post."

That small change can dramatically improve the quality of the output.

Save Your Best Workflows

Every time Claude helps you solve a recurring problem, don't let that workflow disappear inside an old conversation.

Save the prompt, process, or template. Over time, you'll build your own AI playbook. Then you stop starting from zero every time you open Claude. The biggest productivity gain doesn't come from learning hundreds of prompts. It comes from giving AI better context and building systems around the work you repeatedly do. The more Claude understands about your work, the less you have to explain. And that's where the real productivity gains start.
