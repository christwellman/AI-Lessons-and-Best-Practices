## Summary

KDP in 2026 is harder and more mature than in past years. However, the platform remains profitable for publishers who use proper niche research and build intentional catalogs. Unrealistic expectations and poor planning cause most publisher failures.

## Key Takeaways

- KDP market competition increases across almost every niche.
- Barrier to entry is low, but quality requirements are higher now.
- Proper niche research determines whether buyers exist before making a book.
- Book covers must compete side by side with top ranking search results.
- Successful publishers treat KDP like a real business instead of a get rich quick scheme.
- Compound income and seasonal spikes reward publishers who stay patient over time.
